# Reza Alami — Business & Positioning Strategy

How the Reza Alami website becomes a premium client-acquisition platform for the DACH market, starting as a founder-led practice and built to grow into a full agency without a rebuild.

- **Market:** DACH, German-first
- **Model:** Founder-led studio
- **Core offer:** Websites & web apps that sell
- **For:** UI/UX designer · Frontend developer
- **Version:** v1 · October 2026

Design system: see [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md). Prices and conversion figures below are planning assumptions to validate, not market data.

The existing site already has the right visual foundation: dark cinematic, editorial type, restrained motion. The strategy problem is in the words and the structure, not the look.

## 01 · Brand positioning

**The category: websites and web apps that work as a sales system.**

Most DACH SMEs buy their website from a design agency, their SEO from a second vendor and their custom tooling from a third. Nobody owns the result. The combination of production-grade engineering and real marketing experience lets Reza own the whole path from first Google search to closed inquiry. That is the position.

> For owner-led businesses in the DACH region whose website has to bring in customers, not just look good, Reza Alami builds websites and web applications that are designed, engineered and marketed as one system. Unlike design agencies that stop at launch or developers who don't think about sales, every decision is measured against one question: does this bring in more qualified inquiries?
>
> **DE:** Für inhabergeführte Unternehmen im DACH-Raum, deren Website Kunden gewinnen muss: Websites und Web-Apps, die als ein System gestaltet, entwickelt und vermarktet werden. Jede Entscheidung folgt einer Frage: Bringt sie mehr qualifizierte Anfragen?

## 02 · Value proposition

**One accountable partner from strategy to code to ranking.**

You get the strategic thinking of a marketing lead, the craft of a design studio and the engineering of a product team, in one person who is accountable for the outcome. No hand-offs between vendors, no lost context, no finger-pointing when the numbers don't move.

**DE:** Strategie, Design, Entwicklung und Sichtbarkeit aus einer Hand. Ein Ansprechpartner, der für das Ergebnis geradesteht.

| Pillar | Promise | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Build** — Engineered properly | Custom Next.js builds, not page-builder templates. Fast, secure, maintainable, owned by the client. | Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse scores, real web apps in production. |
| **Convert** — Designed to sell | Every page has a job and a measurable goal. Messaging, structure and CTAs come from marketing thinking. | Inquiry and conversion metrics in case studies. |
| **Grow** — Found on Google | Technical SEO built in at code level. Local SEO, structured data and content plans keep traffic growing. | Rankings, organic traffic growth. |
| **Automate** — AI where it pays | Practical AI that saves hours or captures leads: inquiry qualification, assistants, content workflows, process automation. | Hours saved, leads captured. |

**How to talk about AI:** position AI as a capability inside every service, never as a fourth equal pillar on the homepage. DACH SME owners are curious about AI but wary of hype and data-protection risk. "AI-ready" (clean structured data, fast APIs, automation hooks, DSGVO-compliant tooling) is a stronger promise than "AI agency". A dedicated AI service line arrives in phase 2 once there are two or three AI case studies.

## 03 · Service architecture

**Three services lead. Everything else supports or waits.**

A visitor should understand the offer in three items. Each primary service has a clear deliverable, a price floor and a link to proof. Secondary services are mainly sold to existing clients and create recurring revenue. Later services wait until there is team capacity and case-study evidence.

| Service | Tier | What the client buys | Price signal (validate) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Conversion-Website & Relaunch** — Konzept, Design, Entwicklung, SEO-Basis | Lead | A custom website built to generate inquiries, with page strategy, copy guidance, tracking and technical SEO. | ab 7.500 € |
| **Web-Apps & Portale** — Bestellsysteme, Buchung, Kundenportale, interne Tools | Lead | Custom software that replaces manual work or spreadsheets. Supported by Foodigastro24. | ab 15.000 € |
| **SEO & Sichtbarkeit** — Technisches SEO, Local SEO, Content-Plan | Lead | An audit and implementation sprint, then an optional monthly retainer. | Audit ab 990 € · ab 1.200 €/Monat |
| **Wachstumsbetreuung** — CRO, landing pages, analytics, A/B tests | Secondary | Monthly growth retainer for clients after launch. | ab 1.500 €/Monat |
| **KI-Integration & Automatisierung** | Secondary | Inquiry qualification, chat assistants trained on company content, workflow automation. Sold as a project add-on. | ab 2.500 € |
| **Wartung, Hosting & Sicherheit** | Secondary | Care plan: updates, monitoring, backups, small changes. The most important recurring revenue line. | ab 149 €/Monat |
| Performance-Marketing (Google/Meta Ads) | Later | Paid acquisition, once a partner or hire runs it daily. | Phase 2 |
| Branding & Corporate Identity | Later | Logo and identity systems, through a partner designer first. | Phase 2 |

Prices are positioning anchors for the German SME market, not quotes. Showing "ab" prices filters out budget buyers and signals premium before the first call. Adjust to real costs and capacity before launch.

**Productized entry offer — Website-Check (paid, 490 €, credited against a project).** A two-hour analysis of conversion, SEO, speed and tracking, delivered as a recorded video walkthrough plus a written action list. It lowers the risk of the first purchase, qualifies serious buyers and usually turns into a project proposal. A free 5-minute version serves as the lead magnet (see section 08).

## 04 · Target audience

**Owner-led businesses where one new customer pays for the website.**

The best clients have high customer lifetime value, a decision maker you can talk to directly, and a website that is clearly holding them back.

| Segment | Profile | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary — Established SMEs** | 10–250 employees, ~1–50 M € revenue, owner or managing director decides. Website 5+ years old or built on a page builder. | 7.5–40 k € |
| **Primary — Growing service businesses** | 2–20 people, ambitious, earning from referrals and now want inbound leads. | 5–15 k € + monthly retainer |
| **Secondary — Funded founders & startups** | Need an MVP, portal or product site fast and done properly. Good for web-app and AI work. | 15–60 k € |

### Industries, in launch order

| Industry | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Gastronomie, Lieferdienste, Food-Brands | Existing proof (Power Bowls, Foodigastro24). Ordering, booking and local SEO are high-value problems. |
| Lokale & regionale Dienstleister | Proof via Rundumdienst. Cleaning, facility, trades, removals: high search demand, weak competitor sites. |
| Praxen & Kliniken | Dentists, aesthetics, physio. High patient value, booking flows, strict trust and data-protection needs. |
| B2B-Dienstleister & Industrie-Mittelstand | Long sales cycles, large deals, outdated sites. Phase 2, after one B2B case study. |
| Immobilien & Kanzleien | High transaction value, lead-gen friendly. Phase 2. |

### Problems the site must name

- "Our website looks fine, but nobody contacts us through it."
- "We're not on Google's first page for our own city and service."
- "The site is slow, built on WordPress plugins, and nobody dares touch it."
- "Orders, bookings and inquiries still arrive by phone, email and WhatsApp, and we copy them by hand."
- "We pay a designer, an SEO person and a developer, and none of them owns the result."
- "We know AI matters, but we don't know where it would actually save money."
- "We spend on ads but can't see what any of it brings in."

### Who to filter out

- Budgets under 3,000 € looking for "just a simple site".
- Buyers comparing against Wix or a 500 € marketplace offer.
- Projects without a decision maker in the room.

## 05 · Homepage business structure

**The order in which a buyer needs to be convinced.**

The homepage follows the questions a cautious German business owner asks in sequence: Is this for me? Can they do it? Have they done it for someone like me? How does it work? Can I trust this person? What do I do next? The numbering is the real section order.

1. **Hero: outcome promise and two CTAs.** *„Moderne Websites, Apps und KI-Erlebnisse, die Kunden überzeugen."* Sub-line names the audience and the three services. Primary CTA "Erstgespräch vereinbaren", secondary "Projekte ansehen". Below the buttons, one line of micro-proof: response within 24 hours, number of live projects, DACH-based.
2. **Proof strip.** Client logos or project names with one hard number each (Lighthouse score, inquiry growth, orders processed). Show only real figures.
3. **The problem.** Three short pain statements from section 04, in the client's words.
4. **Leistungen: the three lead services.** One block each: outcome headline, what's included, "ab" price, link to service page. A quiet line underneath links to secondary services.
5. **Ausgewählte Projekte.** Two or three case studies, each as challenge → solution → result, using the existing device-screenshot system. Results first, visuals support them.
6. **Der Unterschied.** Comparison table: typical agency vs. cheap freelancer vs. page builder vs. Reza, across ownership, speed, SEO, accountability and price.
7. **Ablauf: the process.** Analyse → Konzept → Design & Entwicklung → Launch → Wachstum. Each step shows duration and what the client receives.
8. **Founder section.** Real photo, two short paragraphs, the story behind the four disciplines: software engineering, AI, web & app development and digital marketing. The strongest trust element in phase 1.
9. **Testimonials.** Two or three quotes with full name, role, company and photo. Video beats text. Never anonymous.
10. **Lead offer.** Free Website-Schnellcheck: URL field plus email, „Sie erhalten innerhalb von 48 Stunden ein persönliches Video." The safety net for visitors not ready to talk.
11. **FAQ.** Cost, timelines, code ownership, EU hosting, DSGVO, after-launch support, remote work. Doubles as FAQ structured data.
12. **Final CTA and footer.** Repeat the primary CTA: „30 Minuten, kostenlos, ohne Verkaufsdruck." Footer with Impressum, Datenschutz, contact details, languages.

### CTA strategy: one goal, three intent levels

| Visitor intent | CTA | Where | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ready to buy | Erstgespräch vereinbaren (30 min, calendar booking) | Header (persistent), hero, after case studies, final CTA | Booked call |
| Has a project, not ready to call | Projekt anfragen (short qualifying form) | Contact page, service pages | Qualified written inquiry |
| Researching | Kostenloser Website-Schnellcheck | Mid-page section, blog posts, end of service pages | Email + URL, nurtured to a call |

Only one CTA is visually primary on any screen. All three routes end in the same place: a conversation with Reza.

## 06 · Personal portfolio vs. future agency

**A founder-led studio: the brand scales, the face stays.**

Confusion happens when a site can't decide whether it is a person or a company. The answer is an endorsed brand: the studio is the container, Reza is the guarantee. Phase 1 and 2: dotMind leads, „by Reza Alami" is the endorsement and Reza is the face. Phase 3: the endorsement drops and Reza appears as founder. The structure stays the same; only the weight shifts.

### Rules that keep it honest and clear

- Write in "ich" in phase 1. A solo founder writing "wir" is noticed quickly by German buyers and costs trust. Switch to "wir" the day a second person regularly works on client projects.
- Never show stock-photo teams or invented headcount. Show a "trusted network" of named specialists only when they exist.
- Turn the About page into a founder page: why you started, how you work, what you refuse to do. No CV-style timeline at the top.
- Projects are the studio's projects. Credit collaborators by name; it makes the studio look bigger, honestly.
- Keep a personal channel (LinkedIn) as Reza's voice. The website speaks for the studio.

### Information architecture that won't need a rebuild

| `/leistungen/[service]` | 1 |
| `/projekte/[slug]` | 1 |
| `/ueber` (founder → studio) | 1 |
| `/kontakt`, `/website-check` | 1 |
| `/impressum`, `/datenschutz` | 1 |
| `/branchen/[industry]` | 2 |
| `/wissen/[article]` (insights) | 2 |
| `/team`, `/karriere` | 3 |

## 07 · Premium positioning

**Premium is shown through restraint and evidence.**

- **Selectivity — show that you choose clients.** „Ich übernehme maximal vier neue Projekte pro Quartal." Show availability („Nächster freier Projektstart: Januar"). Scarcity that is true is the strongest premium signal.
- **Price anchors — publish "ab" prices.** Buyers who see a floor price self-select. Hiding prices looks like negotiating on price.
- **Depth over breadth — fewer, deeper case studies.** Three thorough case studies with numbers beat twelve thumbnails. Show the thinking: research, sketches, decisions, results.
- **The site is the proof — be measurably fast.** Show the site's own Core Web Vitals and Lighthouse score in the footer or on a „So ist diese Website gebaut" page.
- **Language — talk about business results.** „Mehr Anfragen", „weniger Handarbeit", „auf Seite 1 in Ihrer Stadt". Keep tech lists out of sales pages; put them in case-study details.
- **German trust standards — get the legal polish right.** Clean Impressum, written Datenschutz, EU hosting, self-hosted fonts, cookieless analytics, AV-Vertrag on request.
- **Process artefacts — show how the work is delivered.** Example proposal, sample timeline, what a launch handover includes.
- **What to avoid:** skill bars and percentages, "passionate developer", stock photos, twelve services, emoji, a contact form with only "Message", discount offers, unverifiable claims.

## 08 · Client acquisition

**Traffic in, qualified conversations out.**

### The funnel

| Stage | Channel or mechanism | What the site must provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attract | Local and service SEO („Webdesign Ratingen", „Bestellsystem Restaurant"), Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, referrals from clients, partner agencies and tax advisors | Service pages; in phase 2, industry and city pages with real content. Insight articles that answer buyer questions. |
| Convince | Case studies, comparison table, founder section, testimonials | Numbers, faces, named clients, visible process. |
| Capture | Calendar booking, qualifying form, Website-Schnellcheck | Low-friction forms, instant confirmation, a clear statement of what happens next. |
| Qualify | Form fields: project type, budget range, timeline, current URL | Budget ranges starting at 5 k €. Filters mismatches without a conversation. |
| Nurture | Follow-up email sequence after the Schnellcheck (3–4 emails over two weeks: case study, cost FAQ, invitation to a call) | Email tool connected to the form, double opt-in for DSGVO. |
| Expand | Care plan and growth retainer offered at launch, review request 30 days after launch | Care-plan page, testimonial request process. |

### Trust elements, by priority

1. Named case studies with measured results
2. Testimonials with full name, company and photo (video where possible)
3. Founder photo and personal story
4. Visible response promise: „Antwort innerhalb von 24 Stunden"
5. Process with timelines and deliverables per step
6. Google reviews widget once there are 5+ reviews
7. Data-protection signals: EU hosting, DSGVO, no tracking cookies
8. Ownership promise: „Code und Inhalte gehören Ihnen"
9. Performance proof of the site itself
10. Clean Impressum with a real business address

### Offers and lead magnets

- **Website-Schnellcheck (free).** URL in, personal 5-minute video audit out within 48 hours. Highest-converting magnet for this market because it is personal and shows expertise immediately.
- **Website-Kostenrechner.** Interactive estimator (pages, features, languages, integrations) ending in a price range and a booking prompt. Strong SEO asset and a showcase for dev skills.
- **Relaunch-Checkliste (PDF).** 30 points to check before a relaunch, including SEO migration risks.
- **KI-Potenzial-Check für KMU (phase 2).** Short questionnaire that identifies where automation would save time.
- **Website-Check (paid, 490 €).** The bridge from free magnet to project, credited against the build.

### Metrics to track from day one

Visitors → Schnellcheck requests → booked calls → proposals → signed projects. Rough benchmarks for a specialized B2B service site: 1.5–3 % of visitors start an inquiry, 30–50 % of calls lead to a proposal. Use cookieless analytics (Plausible or self-hosted Matomo) with conversion goals on every CTA, so the site runs without a consent banner.

## 09 · Phased roadmap

**Each phase ends with a business trigger, not a date.**

### Phase 1 · months 0–6 — Founder-led portfolio

Goal: prove the offer and fill the pipeline.

- German site at the root URL under `dotmind.de`, dotMind wordmark with „by Reza Alami"
- Three lead services with dedicated pages and "ab" prices
- Three case studies with outcomes (existing projects, completed)
- Booking, qualifying form, Website-Schnellcheck
- Impressum, Datenschutz, cookieless analytics, structured data
- Google Business Profile and first five reviews
- Care plan offered on every launch

**Move on when:** two or more qualified inquiries per week, a steady 8–10 k € monthly revenue, and a first regular collaborator.

### Phase 2 · months 6–18 — Portfolio + agency hybrid

Goal: recurring revenue and a brand bigger than one person.

- dotMind keeps leading; the founder section shrinks and a network strip is added
- Switch to "wir"; network page with named specialists
- Industry pages for the two best verticals
- Insights section with one in-depth article per month
- KI-Integration promoted to a full service with case studies
- English version for international and expat clients
- Kostenrechner launched; growth retainer packaged

**Move on when:** recurring revenue covers fixed costs, two or more people work on client projects every week, demand exceeds capacity.

### Phase 3 · month 18+ — Agency growth platform

Goal: a company that sells without the founder in every conversation.

- Team, careers and culture pages
- Full service menu, including paid media and branding
- Resource hub: guides, tools, webinars
- Arabic and Persian versions (RTL) for diaspora business markets
- Client portal for project status, invoices and reports
- Partner and referral program
- Productized AI offerings for chosen industries

**Success looks like:** most leads arrive without Reza's personal involvement, and the founder sells strategy instead of hours.
